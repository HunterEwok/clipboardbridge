{{t "What it does"}}

{{t "Computer → computer"}}{{t "Copy on one, paste on another. Automatic."}}
{{t "Computer → Android"}}{{t "Copy on the computer, paste on the phone. Automatic."}}
{{t "Android → computer"}}{{t "Copy on the phone; it is sent by itself with the accessibility service, or with one tap."}}

{{t "iOS does not let apps read the clipboard in the background, so the iPhone needs one gesture. On Linux it works with any desktop, and without one in tmux."}}

{{t "Two ways to build your group"}}

{{t "Your devices form a group. Only devices you let in belong to it, and they talk only to each other. A group can have a computer at its heart, or only phones: both are fully supported. We will be honest, though: with a computer everything is simpler."}}

{{t "Simplest"}}

{{t "With a computer"}}

  • {{t "The phone can still hold the group's key in its security chip and admit every device."}}

  • {{t "At home every device syncs directly with the others."}}
  • {{t "Away from home the phone is the group's hub while its app is open; on Android it can keep running in the background."}}

{{t "Why a computer is simpler: it is on far more often than a phone app is open, so sync away from home just works, and the iPhone gives an app little time in the background. When a computer that is always on joins a group of phones, the app offers to make it the hub."}}

{{t "Principles"}}

{{t "Your network only"}}

{{t "At home the devices talk to each other directly. Away from home they reach your group through its own encrypted tunnel. Nothing goes through a cloud."}}

{{t "Paired, then encrypted"}}

{{t "A device joins your group once, by a QR code or a six-digit code. All traffic uses TLS, and devices talk only to the devices of the group."}}

{{t "Keys in the chip"}}

{{t "In a group made on a phone, the phone holds the group's key in its security chip, and each use asks for your face, fingerprint or passcode."}}

{{t "Passwords stay put"}}

{{t "What password managers mark as secret is not synced automatically."}}

{{t "Small"}}

{{t "One file per platform and no dependencies. It runs even on a Raspberry Pi Zero."}}
