{{t "Your clipboard"}}

  • {{t "There are no accounts, no cloud and no telemetry. Clipboard Bridge does not report to anyone."}}
  • {{t "Only devices you let in belong to your group, and they talk only to each other. Every connection is encrypted with TLS, and each device proves it is in the group."}}
  • {{t "A phone keeps its keys in its security chip (the Secure Enclave, Android's Keystore); they never leave it. A computer keeps its keys in files that only Clipboard Bridge can read."}}
  • {{t "What password managers mark as secret is not synced automatically."}}
